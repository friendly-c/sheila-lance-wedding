A rough running order

Here's how
the day goes.

A few key times so you know where to be. The only strict rule is that you have a good time.

Saturday 9 January 2027
1:00 pm

Guests arrive

Come on in, get comfortable, find a familiar face and ease into the day. You’re right on time for the good stuff.

2:00 pm

The I-do bit

Take a seat for the main event: two people, some very important words, and quite possibly a happy tear or twelve. Keep phones tucked away and hearts switched on.

2:30 pm

Reception & celebrations

Time for the cheers, hugs, photos and first round of congratulations. Clink a glass, find your people and let the celebrating commence.

3:30 pm

Time to dine

Take your seats and bring your appetite. The menu is ready, the table chat is warming up, and seconds are a perfectly respectable conversation starter.

5:00 pm

Speeches & happy sniffles

A few stories, a few surprises and some words from the people who know them best. Blame any watery eyes on the lighting.

6:00 pm

Evening guests arrive

The party gets a fresh wave of lovely people. Full-day guests, this is your chance to say hello again; evening guests, come straight in and join the fun.

7:00 pm

First dance

One song, two newlyweds, all the feels. Then the floor is open to everyone, including the people who claim they “don’t really dance”.

8:00 pm

Cake & cheese

A little sweet, a little savoury, and absolutely no need to pick a side. Pace yourself — this is a two-plate opportunity.

11:00 pm

Carriages

One last hug, a final “that was brilliant”, and time to head home. Please plan your journey in advance so the only thing left to organise is your next-day story.
