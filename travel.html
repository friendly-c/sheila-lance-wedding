It's not the destination (but it helps)

The way
to the party.

We're celebrating at The Mill at Elstead. Here's the address, parking information and a few pointers for planning your journey.

The venue

The Mill at Elstead

Farnham Road, Elstead, Godalming, Surrey GU8 6LE

Venue photograph courtesy of The Mill at Elstead.

Open in Google Maps ↗Visit the venue website ↗
Option 01

By car

From the A3, take the B3001 exit and follow the signs for Elstead. The Mill is on Farnham Road.

Parking: there's ample parking at the venue, with spaces in both the main and overflow car parks.

Option 02

By train

Farnham and Godalming stations are both around five miles from The Mill. From either station, you'll need to arrange a taxi for the last part of the journey.

Tip: book your onward taxi in advance, and remember to arrange your return journey too.

Option 03

By bus

There is a limited bus service through Elstead, including route 46. Services are not frequent, so check the operator's latest timetable before travelling.

Worth knowing: plan a separate way home rather than relying on a late bus.

Option 04

Heading home

Please make your own transport arrangements for the journey there and back. If you're using a taxi, we recommend booking it in advance.

Carriages: the celebrations finish at 11:00 pm, so make sure your return plan is sorted before the day.
