A few practical bits, because romance is lovely but knowing what time to arrive is also lovely.

What's the date and when should I arrive?

It's Saturday 9 January 2027. Guests are welcome from 1:00 pm, with the ceremony planned for 2:00 pm. The day page has the full running order.

Can I bring a plus-one or my children?

Your invitation link shows who's included in your party. Please RSVP for the people listed there so we can get the headcount right. If something has changed, contact the couple before submitting.

What should I wear?

Festive, colourful and comfortable. We love a bit of personality, but please avoid white or ivory outfits. A layer for the evening and shoes that work on grass are a good shout.

Full dress-code notes ↗

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes — please add allergies, dietary needs or other important food notes in the RSVP form. We'll confirm the final menu and contact you directly if we need to clarify anything.

Where is The Mill, and is there parking?

We're celebrating at The Mill at Elstead, Farnham Road, Elstead, Godalming, Surrey GU8 6LE. There's ample parking at the venue, with spaces in both the main and overflow car parks. See directions and parking details, or open the location in Google Maps ↗.

Can I get there by train or bus?

Farnham and Godalming are the nearest train stations, both around five miles from the venue, so you'll need to arrange a taxi for the onward journey. There is also a limited bus service through Elstead, including route 46. Please check current timetables and arrange your own return transport in advance, particularly for after the 11:00 pm finish. More travel information ↗

Do you have a gift list?

No gift list, no registry and absolutely no pressure to bring a present. Having you there to celebrate with us is all we could wish for. Just bring yourselves, your hugs and your best dance-floor energy.

Can I take photos?

Absolutely — snap away during the party. We'd love everyone to be present during the ceremony, so please keep phones down for that short bit and let our photographer do their thing.

How do I let you know if I can't make it?

Use your personal RSVP link and choose “Sorry, I can't”. If you have already submitted a response in the prototype, it is locked; for the eventual live site, the couple's contact details will be provided for any changes.

Who do I contact if I'm still unsure?

For this prototype, contact details are placeholders. Before launch, this answer will include the best email or phone number for questions about the day.
